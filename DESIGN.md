## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Response
The design system fuses heritage architectural nobility with contemporary, effortless expedition technology. It radiates warmth, hospitality (*Atithi Devo Bhava*), and discovery without resorting to stereotypical or kitsch tourist clichés. The aesthetic evokes the sensory warmth of spice corridors, carved sandstone jharokhas, and deep twilight over coastal fortresses—translating them into a precise, modern digital interface. The experience feels curated, dependable, tactile, and expansive.

### Design Movement: Modern Heritage & Tactile Warmth
Drawing from a hybrid of **Contemporary Editorial Minimalism** and **Warm Tactile Materiality**, the system avoids cold ultra-flat surfaces in favor of warm stone backdrops, delicate micro-borders, deep lapis-indigo framing, and rich earthen tones. Card decks, subtle debossed containers, and radiant terracotta-to-marigold badges mirror physical print artifacts, archival travel guides, and premium concierge tickets.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
- **Primary (`#E0533C` - Terracotta Vermilion):** Used for primary CTAs, active highlights, key conversion anchors, and interactive focal points. It communicates heat, vitality, and traditional earthen architecture.
- **Secondary (`#0F1B29` - Midnight Peacock):** The foundational dark tone for structural chrome, persistent navigation, primary typography, and night-sky balance. It anchors the light layout with institutional authority.
- **Tertiary (`#F5A623` - Amber Marigold):** Reserved for celebratory accents, ratings, season availability tags, circuit badges, and warm focal pips.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` - Sandstone Linen):** The base background. Surfaces step into `#F4EFEB` for secondary container levels and `#FFFFFF` for elevated foreground cards.
- **Border / Hairline Neutral (`#E8E0D5` / `#DDD3C4`):** Soft, non-aggressive stone tones providing structural clarity without stark contrast.
- **Text Tiers:**
  - *Title / High Emphasis:* `#0F1B29`
  - *Body / Medium Emphasis:* `#2D3A4B`
  - *Muted / Caption:* `#677382`

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys literary stature, historical weight, and premium lifestyle framing. Used for hero statements, destination names, itinerary day headings, and editorial narratives.
- **Interface & Content (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Offers high legibility across dense travel logistics (timetables, fare breakdowns, filter sheets, amenities, and transit stats) without competing against the editorial headers.
- **Regional & Circuit Labels:** Styled using `label-sm` in all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.06em`) to echo high-end cartography and museum badges.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 80px outer margins (`margin-lg`) and 32px gutters (`gutter-lg`). Container max-width is constrained to `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 48px margins (`margin-md`) and 24px gutters (`gutter`). Itinerary cards and gallery views scale fluidly.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with 24px margins (`margin`) and 16px gutters (`gutter-sm`). Horizontal scrolling reels are enabled with peek gutters for circuit discovery chips and seasonal package cards.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built upon an 8pt base unit (subdivided to 4pt for micro-tags and chips). Component interiors consistently employ `space-lg` (24px) for card padding to maintain an open, luxurious breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Model: Atmospheric Sunlight & Grounded Sandstone
Depth avoids artificial, neutral gray drops. Shadows are tinted with midnight blue (`#0F1B29`) and warm ochre, creating soft natural illumination reminiscent of ambient sunlight over stone surfaces.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FBF9F5` base plane.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Low Card):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E0D5`, shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 27, 41, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 27, 41, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Raised Card):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 27, 41, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 27, 41, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Drawer / Itinerary Modal):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 27, 41, 0.16)`.
- **Tactile Inner Polish:** Featured itinerary blocks and search widgets include a delicate inner highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` and `inset 0 -1px 0 rgba(232, 224, 213, 0.4)` to simulate chamfered paper cardstock.

## Shapes

### Form Language
The visual system leverages `roundedness: 2` (16px / `1rem` base border radius for primary containers and cards).
- **Cards & Booking Enclosures:** `16px` (`rounded-lg`) creates an inviting, tactile enclosure without becoming cartoonish.
- **Pills & Chips (Circuits & Badges):** Full pill (`9999px`) for regional classifications, tags, and small utility toggles.
- **Input Fields & Action Controls:** `10px` to `12px` to balance modern structure with soft tactile touch targets.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta Vermilion (`#E0533C`) background, white text, 12px radius, font weight 600. Includes subtle warm shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.28)`. Hover transitions to `#CA432D` with an elevation lift.
- **Secondary / Royal Indigo:** Midnight Peacock (`#0F1B29`) background, white text, paired with primary buttons inside booking actions.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** `#FBF9F5` background with `1px solid #DDD3C4` border, `#0F1B29` text, providing a refined, book-like border.

### 2. Circuit Badges & Discovery Chips
- Used for regional classification (e.g., *Golden Triangle*, *Western Ghats*, *Konkan Coast*, *Buddhist Circuit*).
- Height: 28px, pill-shaped (`9999px`).
- Background: Sand tint (`#F4EFEB`) or light saffron tint (`rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.14)`).
- Text: `#0F1B29` or Terracotta, uppercase `label-sm`, letter spacing `0.06em`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.2)`.

### 3. Tour & Itinerary Cards
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) sitting on `#FBF9F5` sand neutral.
- Radius: `16px` (`rounded-lg`).
- Border: `1px solid #E8E0D5`.
- Image aspect ratio: 16:10 with smooth image zoom on parent hover.
- Inner layout: Generous 20px padding, typography-led hierarchy (Days/Nights tag, destination serif title, key inclusions row, and price anchor).

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `#FFFFFF`.
- Border: `1px solid #DDD3C4`. Focus state transitions to `1.5px solid #E0533C` with an amber focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.12)`.
- Floating search bar widget (Hero): Floating elevated pill/rounded container (`20px` radius) with interior dividers tinted in `#E8E0D5`.

### 5. Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radio / Checkbox unselected: `1.5px solid #C4B9AA` on white.
- Checked state: Terracotta (`#E0533C`) fill with crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### 6. Specialized Regional & Travel Components
- **Day Timeline Node:** Vertical Sandstone line (`2px solid #E8E0D5`) connecting days, anchored by an Amber Marigold (`#F5A623`) circular pin.
- **Seasonal Availability Meter:** Pill indicator showing peak, moderate, and monsoon travel windows using warm amber and deep indigo iconography.
